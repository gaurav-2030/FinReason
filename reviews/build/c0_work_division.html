How to prepare

  • Read your part of the review's Speaker script: what to say on each of your slides, with the hand-over line to the next presenter.
  • Learn your slides' “Key point” lines first — they are the sentence to stress on each slide.
  • Then read the “Learn” column of your review's notes and practise the questions listed under “Answers”.
  • Everyone should be able to explain three things in one sentence: the scoring rule (within 0.5%), Program-of-Thought (Python does the maths) and VPSC (reject programs using numbers not in the report).
  • Dhruvagouda runs the live demo in Review 3; Gaurav keeps the slides with screenshots ready as a backup.
